clipfarm – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

clipfarm is a personal, non-commercial tool run by a single individual on their own computer. It prepares short gaming highlight videos and sends them to the operator's own social media accounts (YouTube, Instagram, TikTok). It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Data we access

  • The operator's own accounts. With the operator's consent (OAuth), clipfarm uploads videos to the operator's own accounts (on TikTok, as drafts that the operator reviews and posts manually) and reads basic statistics of those videos (views, likes, shares, retention).
  • Public data. clipfarm reads publicly available information through official APIs (Twitch Helix, YouTube Data API): live stream lists, public clips and public video metadata.

Data we do not collect

clipfarm does not collect, store, sell or share personal data of any third party. It has no user accounts, no analytics, no cookies and no advertising.

Storage and security

Access tokens and statistics are stored only on the operator's own computer and are never transmitted to any server other than the official API of the platform they belong to.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the security settings of each platform (Google Account permissions, TikTok "Manage app permissions", Instagram "Apps and websites").

YouTube

clipfarm uses YouTube API Services. By using it, the operator agrees to the YouTube Terms of Service; see also the Google Privacy Policy.
